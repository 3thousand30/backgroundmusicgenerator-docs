# Privacy

Background Music Generator stores its settings and Music API Profiles on your Windows device. API keys are encrypted for your Windows account.

When you generate music, BMG sends the brief and selected output settings directly to the provider in your active Music API Profile. BMG does not operate a music-generation service, hold your provider balance, or keep a cloud library of your generated tracks.

Generated audio is saved to the output folder you choose. Provider privacy, retention, and commercial-use terms are governed by that provider.
