# Output Templates

Templates are reusable output starting points. Unlike a persona, a template describes the job for this track: for example, a Product Demo or Podcast Intro.

## Included templates

- Calm Voiceover Background
- Product Demo
- Study & Focus
- Documentary
- Podcast Intro
- Creator Video

Choosing a template fills practical defaults such as duration, mood, genre, tempo, instruments, loop setting, and output format. The template does not lock those values: the settings visible in **2. Output** are used for the current track.

## Edit a template

On **2. Output**, use the pencil beside the template picker. You can rename the template, change its description and prompt framework, and adjust its defaults and do/avoid rules. Mood is a multi-select list and genre is a list, matching the controls used when you generate.

The prompt framework and do/avoid rules remain part of the template. The Output controls override only the matching defaults—duration, mood, genre, tempo, instruments, loop setting, and output format—for this track.

Use personas for a recurring musical direction and templates for a recurring deliverable. For example, Brand & Product Music can pair with a Product Demo template.
